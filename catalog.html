Product Number Scheme

Every PWG model number follows a consistent, decodable structure.

PWG – ARCH – OD – TERM – LEN – [-NM] – [-LN]
Segment Meaning
PWGCompany / technology
ARCHProduct family — C1, P1, or X1
ODOuter diameter, inches (4 digits)
TERMTermination — see Connector Options
LENLength in millimeters (4 digits)
-NMNon-Magnetic option (optional suffix)
-LNLow-Noise option (optional suffix)

Standard Lengths

  • 0150 = 150 mm
  • 0200 = 200 mm
  • 0250 = 250 mm
  • 0300 = 300 mm
  • 0330 = 330 mm
  • CUSTOM = custom length

Custom lengths use the actual manufactured length in millimeters, e.g. 0742.
