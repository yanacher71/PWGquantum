Cryogenic Interconnect Infrastructure

PWG focuses on the interconnect infrastructure that links superconducting quantum processors to the classical control electronics that operate them. As systems move from single-chip experiments to multi-stage cryostats with rising channel counts, the cabling and connector infrastructure between temperature stages becomes a limiting factor in its own right.

Our platforms are engineered end-to-end — from connector interface to cable routing — to hold signal integrity across that temperature gradient, while remaining practical to install, service, and scale inside real cryostat systems. The specific materials and internal construction behind each platform are proprietary; what we share publicly is how each product performs and integrates into your system.
